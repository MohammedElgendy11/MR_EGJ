## 2024-05-24 - Tactile VR Button Feedback
**Learning:** VR buttons without visual or tactile feedback feel unresponsive and confusing to users, especially when they only rely on pointer interactions. In `KeypadButton` and `HintButton`, users could not easily distinguish when a press registered successfully.
**Action:** Implemented a standard coroutine-based scale animation (scaling down to 0.9x and back to 1.0x over 0.1s) to provide immediate visual "squish" feedback upon interaction. This pattern should be consistently applied to all interactive flat UI components in VR environments to ensure accessible and clear interaction states. Initial scale must always be cached in `Awake` and restored in `OnDisable` to avoid breaking custom Inspector scales or leaving buttons stuck in a pressed state when deactivated mid-animation.
