## 2024-05-24 - [VR Button Tactile Feedback]
**Learning:** VR buttons without physical tactile feedback often leave users unsure if their input was registered, leading to repeated presses and frustration.
**Action:** Implemented a standardized coroutine-based scale animation (scaling to 0.9x over 0.05s then back to original scale over 0.05s) for UI buttons to provide immediate visual confirmation of interaction.
